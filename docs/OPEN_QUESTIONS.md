# Open questions and decision gates

| Question | Accountable decision / consultation |
| --- | --- |
| What data fields and status semantics are authorized for each department? | Department data owners and Operations |
| How are turnaround, reopenings, cancellations, backlog and reporting periods defined? | Operations and departmental owners |
| What are each team's targets and effective dates? | Department owners and leadership |
| Can the third-party ticketing system expose an approved API or export? | IT system owner |
| What stable interfaces exist for Excel, SharePoint and Facilities mailbox? | Source owners and architect |
| What connector authentication methods, tenant policies and licenses are available? | IT/security and licensing owner |
| Which identity boundaries, credential controls and review cadence are appropriate? | Security and platform owner |
| What refresh cutoff, quality thresholds and incident response expectations apply? | Operations and source stewards |
| What retention, aggregation and detail-access rules are approved? | Security, compliance and data owners |
| Is Power Apps needed, or would reporting plus configuration tools meet the business need? | Architect recommendation, sponsor approval |
| Which data platform and ingestion/reporting services meet needs at acceptable cost? | Architect recommendation after proof of concept |
| Which environments and funding are available for DEV/UAT/PROD simulation? | Platform owner and sponsor |

No architecture option is accepted until its dependencies and trade-offs are understood.
